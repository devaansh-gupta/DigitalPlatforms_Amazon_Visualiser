03 / Framework 2

Coopetition: partner, intermediary and competitor

X Fire and Amazon cooperate in distribution while potentially competing for overlapping product sales.

Three roles

Sellers expand selection and pay for services; Amazon benefits from marketplace participation and more product variety.

06 / SWOT synthesis

What X Fire can control—and what it cannot

SWOT consolidates the diagnosis; it does not replace the causal analysis above.

X Fire case + interpretation

STRENGTHS

Specialist category knowledge; physical-store experience; existing marketplace sales and operating know-how.

WEAKNESSES

High Amazon revenue concentration; limited bargaining power relative to the platform.

OPPORTUNITIES

Alternative channels; differentiated bundles or proprietary products; stronger supplier relationships; selective geographic expansion.

THREATS

Direct competition; concentration risk; fee and visibility exposure; limited control over some marketplace mechanisms.

Opportunities are options to test, not guaranteed commercial outcomes. Case exhibits do not provide enough information to assume that every alternative channel will be profitable.

07 / Strategic pivot

Unbundle the relationship

Amazon's May 2026 announcement of Amazon Supply Chain Services provides a new lens: evaluate logistics separately from the place where customers buy. Availability, terms, route coverage and costs must be confirmed for a real implementation.

Beyond the case
MODEL A

All-in Amazon

Fast access to demand and fulfilment; greatest concentration of sales-channel dependence.

Concentrated control
MODEL B

Multi-channel + Amazon logistics

Diversify where orders originate while using Amazon logistics where terms and economics fit.

Separate the modules
MODEL C

Multi-channel + multi-provider

More coordination and complexity, but stronger fallback options and flexibility.

More optionality
Recommendation for X Fire: preserve Amazon sales where contribution economics justify them, negotiate with suppliers based on X Fire's own commercial value, and run bounded experiments in alternative customer and fulfilment channels. Do not treat Amazon.ca expansion as diversification away from Amazon.
08 / Decision and implementation

Scale without surrendering control

The objective is not to leave Amazon at any cost. It is to preserve profitable participation while making the brand more resilient to change.

Recommended path
DAYS 0–30 · MEASURE

Know the economics

Calculate contribution margin by product and channel. Map sales, supplier, advertising and fulfilment concentration.

DAYS 31–60 · TEST

Build alternatives

Test a direct channel, a second marketplace, a differentiated offer or an alternative logistics provider on a bounded scale.

DAYS 61–90 · SCALE

Expand what works

Compare contribution profit, customer-acquisition cost, repeat purchases, service levels and working capital before scaling.
